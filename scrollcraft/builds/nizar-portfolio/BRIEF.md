# BRIEF: Nizar Kadri portfolio

Interviewed. Verbatim answers are quoted; everything marked **Authored** is a
decision made under the owner's delegation ("be creative", "leave them to your
imagination", "if you think of anything better go for it").

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe and references.** "dark", "a film". Supplied references: a tower of
   floating doors and frames above a lone figure, a black chess king glowing in
   fog on a floating board, a sculpted pastel relief with a warm neon seam, a
   white spotlit podium. **Authored:** cold, quiet, nocturnal, deliberate, one
   warm light.
2. **Journey.** "Landing page must be shockingly impressive ... inspiration from
   chess (I have been playing chess since childhood and I am really good at it,
   I wanna make user feel the connection of intelligence in chess and coding and
   designing and solving problems). The next a glance to my projects and
   experience which they can explore more by navigating to dedicated pages. The
   next would be the about section which I would keep separate from landing
   page. All from start to end the user should feel the urge to reach out to me."
3. **Energy curve.** **Authored:** quiet open, rising through the work, a held
   silence, the loudest moment at the tower, then stillness at the close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** "The tower ... if you think of anything
   better than tower go for it."
5. **What no other site does.** **Authored:** the page is a chess game the
   visitor plays by scrolling, and its last move is theirs.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** "more premium", "dark". **Authored:**
   premium cinematic nocturne, not minimal: a full 3D world, dense atmosphere.
7. **One world or scenes.** "connected worlds but the core theme revolves around
   tech, ai, chess, toronto and a bit of nature, it would be really impressive
   if you can come up with a world that involves it all".
8. **Assets.** Four reference images (inspiration only, recreated, not shipped).
   Four LinkedIn certificate PDFs now, "total 15 certificates which I will add
   later" (placeholders). Portraits: "choose one or two max". Higgsfield
   generation authorised mid-build; kie.ai is never called.

Scope: "Entire website". Project library: "3d scrollable animations with each
project having a title tech stack and if available a live preview". Project
covers: "more polished covers which you need to generate".

## The world: The Long Game (authored)

One place. An endless chessboard of black stone at the edge of Lake Ontario at
night. The pieces stand at the scale of buildings, and past the far rank the
board becomes Toronto: the CN Tower stands among the towers like one more piece.
Circuit traces are etched into the squares and carry warm light when a thought
passes through them (tech, AI). Fog, black water, a pine shoreline (nature).
Everything is cold blue-black except one warm ember light: the lit king, the
lit doors, the square that is waiting for a move.

Style preamble (verbatim on every generated asset):

> Cinematic night photography, 35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field.
> Black polished stone chess pieces at architectural scale on a vast dark stone
> chessboard beside a still black lake, heavy low fog, distant city lights.
> One warm ember-orange practical light, cool blue-black ambient fill, deep
> falloff into shadow. Grade: blue-black shadows, bone-white highlights, a
> single ember accent. Wet reflective surfaces, fine film grain, slight
> halation. Photographic realism. No text, no logos, no neon purple, no
> cartoon, no clay, no plastic sheen.

Tokens: canvas `#07090C`, surface `#0F1319`, ink `#ECE7DF`, ink-soft `#98A0AB`,
accent `#FF8A4C` (ember), accent-ink `#140A04`. Display Archivo (expanded,
heavy), text Geist, notation Geist Mono (used only for chess notation and data).

## Grammar

**Continuous world** (uniqueness.md 2.4): one fixed stage for the whole landing
page, copy arriving at waypoints, no section seams. The nav is a map (the
scoresheet), the hero is an establishing position, the close is an arrival and
the CTA is an object in that place (the square waiting for the reply).

Declared deviation: the grammar asks for worldflight video legs. This build uses
one fixed real-time WebGL stage instead, because the doors must carry real
certificates and project covers, respond to the pointer and be clickable, and a
real-time camera cannot have a seam. Generated imagery feeds the world as
textures, plates, covers and posters.

Why the other seven lost: filmic one-shot forbids jumping, and a portfolio
visitor must be able to; chaptered editorial and typographic poster have no room
for a 3D world; live surface needs a product; gallery fits /projects (and is
used there) but not the argument of the landing page; split stage has no second
side; a cutlist cannot hold a peak this long.

## The world: The Long Game, from daylight to blue hour (authored)

One place: a marble-and-glass architecture standing in a sea of cloud. The game
takes a day, so the light changes as the visitor scrolls. It opens on a marble
board in the sky, passes a sculpture of the player dissolving into glass cubes,
enters a dark enclosed hall with one blue portal, crosses a gallery of glass
waves and flowing light, climbs a rock spire at blue hour, and ends above the
cloud spiral at the edge of space with one square lit.

Style preamble (verbatim on every generated asset):

> Cinematic architectural photography, wide-angle lens, deep focus,
> photographic realism. Clean minimal monumental architecture in polished
> marble and clear glass. Light is soft and tightly controlled: no glare, no
> lens flare, no bloom, no halos, no blown highlights, gentle contrast, long
> soft falloff into shadow. Grade: deep blue-black shadows, cool slate grey,
> marble white, one restrained electric cobalt blue accent. Fine film grain. No
> text, no letters, no logos, no people, no watermark, no cartoon, no clay, no
> plastic.

Tokens, two tones on one system. Night: canvas `#070B14`, surface `#0E1524`,
ink `#EEF2F7`, ink-soft `#9AA7B8`, accent `#7FB0FF`. Day (`.tone-day`): canvas
`#F3F1EC`, surface `#FFFFFF`, ink `#10141B`, ink-soft `#566070`, accent
`#1F4FD8`. One solid blue for buttons on either tone: `#2A62F0`. Gold `#B8935A`
is reserved for hairlines. Faces unchanged: Archivo, Geist, Geist Mono.

## Grammar

**Continuous world, in its native form: worldflight.** One fixed stage, seven
video legs scrubbed by scroll, copy in one fixed layer, nothing in document
flow but the spacer. Revision 1 declared a deviation (a real-time WebGL stage);
that deviation was the source of the popping, glare and uneven pace, and it is
withdrawn. Doors, certificates, stack labels and the scoresheet are real DOM on
top of the film, so they are sharp and clickable at any size.

Pace: every 5 s leg owns 1.3 vh and the 10 s tower leg owns 2.6 vh, so the rate
is one number for the whole flight (0.26 vh per second of film).
